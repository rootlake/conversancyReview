converancy practice 3 - naked eye sky phenomena

(Started in class)

Use any AI or other method to find answers and be ready to discuss. Come with good concepts, knowledge, and questions!

Continue to research and review the answer to these questions and others (looking for that 4!) in hopes of Practicing Skillfully as we discuss in class.

1. Why do lunar phases occur? What are all the key vocab words for understanding moon phases. What do each mean? (short answers, not long sentences)

2. Generally speaking (to the nearest hour), when does a full moon rise, reach its peak, and set? A new moon? The two quarters? Try making a table to see the pattern.

3. How did the term 'the dark side of the Moon' come to be? Is there a dark side of the Moon? Explain.

4. What is the name of the event when the Moon passes in front of the Sun as seen by an observer on Earth? What are the types? What phase must the Moon be in for this to occur?

5. What are the different phenomena and observations that occur during the above event?

6. What is the name of the event when the Moon is hidden by the Earth's shadow? What are the types? What phase must the Moon be in for this to occur?

7. What phenomena and observations occur during the above event?

8. What is it called when a different body passes in front of the sun? What objects can do it? When was the last one and when is the next?

9. What's an 'occultation' involving the moon?

10. What do people mean by 'the planets aligning?' What is a planetary conjunction?

11. What are the scientific names for the phenomena people call "shooting stars"? What are the different 'phases' of these objects as they encounter the Earth's atmosphere?

12. What are the more predictable, regular events of shooting stars called? Why are they named the way they are? What is the 'radiant'?

13. What happens when an especially large space rock hits the Earth's atmosphere? What can happen? What phenomena can be seen or experienced?

14. What evidence do we see on the Earth of meteors that hit the ground?

15. How did meteor events change the course of life on Earth? What are the details about the event that caused the most recent dinosaurs to go extinct?
