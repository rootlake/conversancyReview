Conversancy Practice 1

Use these questions to explore the lines, vocab words, and concepts surrounding Earth and our coordinate systems. You can use Google, ChatGPT, or other resources to track these things down.

The goal is not to answer ALL of these questions (at least not yet); the goal is to learn to do enough research to be Conversant (fluent) about some of these topics.

1. If you were standing in Pomfret's quad at dawn, which building or buildings would the sun rise over? (check out Google Maps if you need to)

2. If you were standing in the center of the Pomfret chapel, which way would North be?

3. What is the equator? Does it have real meaning or is it just arbitrary? In other words, does it have to be where it is or could it be anywhere?

4. What is a meridian? Do they have real meaning or are they arbitrary? Explain.

5. What is a great circle? Explain -- real or arbitrary?

6. What is a parallel? Real or arbitrary?

7. What goes through Greenwich, England? How did it come to be? Real or arbitrary?

8. What's the other special line of longitude? What happens if you cross over it?

9. What are Pomfret School's longitude and latitude? What are the units this system uses? What lines are they based on? (see above answers)

10. What is Pomfret's time zone? How many hours is our time different from Universal Time (UT)?

11. What are the equivalents of longitude and latitude in the sky? How do they 'work'? (Find the answers in information on the Equatorial coordinate system)

12. What is the Observer Based system of locating objects in the sky? How does it work?

13. What is the name of the point directly over an observer's head? What is the name of the opposite point, directly beneath an observer, on the surface of the Earth? What about the point opposite the observer in the sky?

14. What are the Tropic of Cancer and the Tropic of Capricorn? Why are they 'special' when it comes to astronomy?

15. What are the Arctic and Antarctic Circles? Why are they special in astronomy?
