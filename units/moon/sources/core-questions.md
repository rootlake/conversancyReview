Conversancy Test 2: The Moon

General topics:

- Moon features
- Moon history and composition
- Moon missions

Core questions:

1. What are the major theories about how the Moon formed, and which is currently favored by scientists?
2. What are the major features of the Moon's surface?
3. What is the Moon made of, inside and out?
4. Why are the near and far sides of the Moon different, particularly in their surface features?
5. What can impact craters tell us about the Moon and the solar system?
6. How many American missions have traveled to the Moon, and what were their objectives and accomplishments?
7. What other countries have sent missions to the Moon, and what have they accomplished?
8. How do we know humans really landed on the Moon?
9. Is there water on the Moon, and why does it matter?
10. What are the biggest unanswered questions about the Moon?

Also covered in class: evidence and considerations for Moon landing hoax theories.
